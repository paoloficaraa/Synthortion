# Design System Inspired by Deadlock — Dark Industrial & Glitch Brutalism

> Category: Brands

> Surface: web

*Dark, Raw & Experimental — High-contrast monochrome aesthetic, bold brutalist typography, VHS/glitch textures, and data-dense layouts built on pure dark surfaces.*

Deadlock is a collective of 11 Italian music producers working with global artists, running an e-commerce platform for sound design assets, drumkits, and presets. Brand Voice: Direct, authentic, unpretentious, and technical when needed ("like a producer in the studio"). Product Context: E-commerce for music producers, digital sound packs, community events, and content. Visual Aesthetic: High-contrast black and white, brutalist typography (Akira Expanded), VHS/glitch textures, and raw underground style.

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| background | Pitch Black | `#000000` | Primary page canvas — deep dark surface for maximum visual depth |
| surface | Elevated Surface | `#121212` | Cards, panels, modals, and container surfaces |
| foreground | Stark White | `#ffffff` | Primary readable text, headlines, and high-impact CTA buttons on dark surfaces |

## Typography
- **Display:** Akira Expanded — weights 800, 900 — fallbacks: Impact, Haettenschweiler, Arial Narrow Bold, sans-serif
- **Body:** Inter — weights 400, 700 — fallbacks: system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono — weights 400, 700 — fallbacks: Space Mono, ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, monospace

## Voice & Tone

- **Adjectives:** direct, raw, unpretentious, technical, underground, authentic
- **Tone:** Direct, raw, unpretentious — like a producer in the studio. Technical when discussing sound (Kits, Presets, BPM, Stems, DSP), authentic and grounded when talking to the community. Concise, uppercase-heavy UI labels.

### Messaging pillars
- E-commerce platform for music producers — digital sound packs (drumkits, presets, stems), community events, and content.
- Brand voice is direct, authentic, unpretentious, and technical when needed — 'like a producer in the studio'.
- Visual aesthetic: high-contrast black and white, brutalist typography (Akira Expanded), VHS/glitch textures, raw underground style.
- Collective of 11 Italian music producers working with global artists.

### Vocabulary
- **Use:** DOWNLOAD KIT, STREAM CREDITS, JOIN, BPM, STEMS, DSP, PRESETS, DRUMKIT, SAMPLE RATE, KEY, WAV, MONO, STEREO
- **Avoid:** soft pastels, warm off-whites, premium quality, hand-crafted, curated, boutique, artisanal, bespoke, cheerful stock copy, corporate polish

## Imagery

- **Style:** Dark, high-contrast monochrome renders; halftone textures; VHS scanlines; pixel-glitch artifacts; raw industrial photography.
- **Subjects:** Digital noise textures, studio equipment close-ups, brutalist architecture, distorted 3D renders, waveform/spectrum visualizations, dark urban environments
- **Treatment:** Strict monochrome or near-monochrome. Heavy contrast, visible grain/noise. Scanline overlays. No color grading toward warm tones.
- **Avoid:** Soft natural light, lifestyle photography, studio headshots, glossy product shots on white backgrounds, warm tones, stock photography, pastel gradients, cheerful stock aesthetics

## Layout

- **Radius:** 2px
- **Border weight:** 1px
- **Spacing:** Spacing scale: 2/4/8/16/24/32/48/64px

### Posture rules
- Strict dark-mode-first: #000000 background, never light canvases.
- Sharp radii only — max 2px border-radius. No rounded or playful UI elements.
- Visible structural grid with 1px solid borders (#333 or #FFF).
- Dense typographic packing for data grids contrasted with large areas of pure dark whitespace.
- Modular block-based layouts with stark 1px solid borders.
- No soft-blur drop shadows — use sharp 1–2px offset borders or pure contrast layering.
- High data density: display technical details (bpm, key, sample rate, file format) alongside visual assets.
- Buttons: Primary = solid #FFF bg + #000 uppercase bold text, 0–2px radius. Secondary = #000 bg + 1px solid #FFF border + white text. Hover triggers full inversion.
- Inputs: Dark fields (#000) with 1px solid #333 border; focus state inverts to solid #FFF bg + #000 text.
- Cards: Dark background (#121212), high-contrast border, optional scanline/status bar with monospace metadata.
